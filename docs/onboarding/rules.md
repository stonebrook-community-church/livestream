# Operating Rules

Every operator follows these six rules, every Sunday. Each rule below says what to do, how to do it and why it exists. Once you know the reason, the rule is easy to remember.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Only move the camera that isn't live. |
| 2 | Lyrics always appear in the lower third, never full screen. |
| 3 | During songs, switch at your judgment, using P1 and P2 on both cameras. |
| 4 | Sunday school dismissal: never show slides. Use Cam 1 Clean, Cam 1 P2. |
| 5 | Communion: Cam 2 Clean, Cam 2 P4. |
| 6 | Problems: find the tech lead. |

## 1. Only move the camera that isn't live

!!! danger "Only move the camera that isn't live"
    Recall the preset on the off-air camera, wait for it to settle, then cut.

**How:**

1. Check which camera is in the scene on air. That camera is **live**.
2. On the SuperJoy, select the other camera, the **off-air** one.
3. Recall the preset you need.
4. Wait until the camera stops moving completely.
5. Press the scene button for that camera, check the preview, then press **Publish**.

Not sure which camera is live? See the table on [Video Basics](video-basics.md#program-and-preview).

**Why:** a PTZ camera moves slowly and smoothly. If it is live while it moves, every viewer watches the picture swing across the room and zoom in. It looks like a mistake, and on a big screen it can make people feel seasick. When you move the off-air camera, nobody sees the move. Viewers only see a clean cut from one finished shot to another.

**Why wait for it to settle:** if you cut while the camera is still moving, viewers see the end of the swing. A camera can look like it has stopped while it is still finishing a slow zoom. Give it a moment.

## 2. Lyrics stay in the lower third

Lyrics always appear in the lower overlay. They are never shown full screen.

**How:** for songs, use Scene 2 — Cam 1 + Lyrics or Scene 3 — Cam 2 + Lyrics. Don't use Scene 6 — Full Slide to show lyrics, even if the lyric bar seems small.

**Why:**

- **Viewers at home want to see the room.** Seeing people lead and sing makes the stream feel like worship instead of a slideshow. A lower third lets viewers read the words and still see the people.
- **It looks the same every week.** Viewers learn where to look for the words.
- **The words stay readable.** The lower-third bar is a fixed size and place, built for the stream. Viewers don't have to hunt for the words.

## 3. Worship switching: P1 and P2, your judgment

During songs, you decide when to switch. Use only P1 and P2, on both cameras, and follow rule 1.

| | Cam 1 (center) | Cam 2 (left side) |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Full stage wide | Side wide of stage |
| **P2** | Worship leader, medium | Band / instruments |

**How:**

1. Start a song on one camera, for example Scene 2 — Cam 1 + Lyrics on Cam 1 P2.
2. While Cam 1 is live, recall P1 or P2 on Cam 2.
3. When it's a good moment, cut to Scene 3 — Cam 2 + Lyrics.
4. Now Cam 1 is off air. Recall its next preset, and repeat.

**Why only P1 and P2:** these four shots are made for music: the whole stage, the worship leader and the band. The other presets point at the pulpit, the announcements spot or the communion table, which are empty or wrong during a song. With only four shots to choose from, you can stay calm and focus on timing.

**Why your judgment:** music doesn't follow a script, so there is no fixed list of cuts. You're the one watching the room, so you decide.

!!! tip "Good habits for switching during songs"
    - Cut at natural breaks: a new verse, the chorus, the end of a line. Avoid cutting in the middle of a word.
    - Hold each shot for at least a few lines. Cutting too often makes the stream feel restless.
    - When in doubt, go wide (P1).

## 4. Sunday school dismissal: never show slides

!!! danger "Sunday school dismissal: never show slides"
    Use Scene 4 — Cam 1 Clean on Cam 1 P2. Don't press Scene 6 — Full Slide or Scene 7 — Sermon Split.

**How:** before the dismissal starts, get Cam 1 onto Cam 1 P2 (see [Sunday Flow](sunday-flow.md#sunday-school-dismissal) for the safe way to do this). Then stay on Scene 4 — Cam 1 Clean until the dismissal is over, even if a slide goes up in the room.

**Why:**

- **The slides at this moment are for the room, not for viewers.** Whatever ProPresenter shows during the dismissal is meant for the children and families in the room.
- **A stage-only shot keeps the children off the stream.** The stream is public. Cam 1 P2 frames only the people on stage, so children walking out of the room stay out of the picture.

## 5. Communion: Cam 2 Clean, Cam 2 P4

Communion happens once a month. Use Scene 5 — Cam 2 Clean on Cam 2 P4.

**How:** while Cam 1 is live in the part before communion, recall Cam 2 P4. Wait for it to settle, then press Scene 5 — Cam 2 Clean and **Publish**.

**Why Cam 2:** the communion table is on the floor in front of the stage. From the back, Cam 1 sees it small at the bottom of the frame. Cam 2 sees it from the side, close enough to fill the frame with the table.

**Why clean:** communion is a quiet moment. Nothing should cover the picture, and there are no lyrics to show.

## 6. Problems: find the tech lead

If something breaks and a scene button can't fix it, find the **tech lead**. They are usually at church on Sunday. If they aren't, their contact details are on the **private booth card** at the booth. They are never on this site.

**How:** first go to a safe scene, then find the tech lead. The steps are on [When Things Go Wrong](when-things-go-wrong.md).

**Why get help instead of fixing it yourself:** you are running the stream alone. If you start trying fixes, nobody is watching the program, and a guess can make things worse. The tech lead knows the system and can often fix the problem quickly while you keep the stream steady.

**Why the contact details aren't on this site:** this site is public. Anyone on the internet can read it. Phone numbers, logins and stream keys on a public page could be misused, for example by someone streaming to our channel. They belong on the printed booth card only.
