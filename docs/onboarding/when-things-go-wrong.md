# When Things Go Wrong

Something will go wrong one day. That's normal, and it isn't your fault. You don't need to fix it. Your job is to keep a calm picture on the stream and get help.

## The three steps

1. **Stay calm.** Viewers at home forgive a few seconds of a still shot. They notice panic: a camera swinging around or scenes flashing.
2. **Go to a safe scene.** Pick one from the table below. Press its button, then **Publish**.
3. **Find the tech lead.** They are usually at church on Sunday. If they aren't, their contact details are on the **private booth card** at the booth.

!!! danger "Only move the camera that isn't live"
    This rule still applies when something is wrong. Cut away first, then fix the camera that is off air.

## Safe scenes

A **safe scene** is one that always looks acceptable, whatever is happening in the room.

| What's wrong | Go to |
|---|---|
| A camera shot is wrong: wrong preset, someone stepped out of frame | The other camera, or **Cam 1 Clean (4)** on **Cam 1 P1** |
| Cam 1 is frozen, black or not responding | **Cam 2 Clean (5)** on **Cam 2 P1** |
| Both cameras have a problem | **Full Slide (6)**. Ask the ProPresenter operator for a suitable slide. |
| A problem before the service starts | Stay on **Countdown (1)** |

P1 is the wide shot on both cameras, so it is never wrong. If Cam 1 P1 is already live, you don't need to move anything.

## Who to tell

| Problem | Tell |
|---|---|
| Wrong lyrics or slide | The ProPresenter operator |
| Sound is missing, too loud or too quiet | The audio team. You don't adjust audio. |
| Picture, stream, Ecamm, Resi, cameras, Stream Deck or SuperJoy | The tech lead |
| Not sure | The tech lead |

## Don't

- **Don't end the Resi stream or stop broadcasting in Ecamm to "reset" things.** Viewers get cut off, and the stream may not come back on its own.
- **Don't move the live camera** to fix a shot. Cut away first.
- **Don't change presets, exposure or scene settings.** Those changes stay for next Sunday. Leave them to the Livestream Lead and the tech lead.
- **Don't try fixes you haven't been trained on.** While you try them, nobody is watching the stream.

## After the service

1. Write down what went wrong, roughly when, and what you did.
2. Tell the Livestream Lead, even if the problem fixed itself. It helps the next operator.

!!! tip "Keep the booth card at the booth"
    The booth card is private and printed. Don't photograph it, copy it into a message or post it anywhere. If it's missing, tell the Livestream Lead.
