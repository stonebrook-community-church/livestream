## Headroom

**Headroom** is the space between the top of the head and the top of the frame. Keep a small gap. Too much makes the person look small. None cuts off the head.

<figure markdown="span">
  ![A person at the pulpit. An arrow marks the small gap between the top of the head and the top of the frame.](../assets/diagrams/cam1-p3.svg)
  <figcaption>The arrow at the top marks the headroom.</figcaption>
</figure>

## Overlay and lower third

An **overlay** is a picture placed on top of the camera shot. A **lower third** is an overlay across the bottom third of the frame. Our lyrics always use a lower third.
