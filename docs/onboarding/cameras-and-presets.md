# Cameras and Presets

Where the cameras are, what each preset shows, the exposure policy, how to use the SuperJoy, and why you only move the off-air camera.

A **preset** is a saved camera position. You recall it with one button on the SuperJoy. For other video terms, see [Video Basics](video-basics.md).
