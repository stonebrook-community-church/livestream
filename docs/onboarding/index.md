# Onboarding Path

There are four steps from your first read of this guide to running a Sunday on your own. Only the Livestream Lead can sign you off.

## The four steps

1. **Read this guide, then do a hands-on session.** Read the pages below in order. Then practice at the booth with a trainer on a day that isn't Sunday, so nothing you try goes out live.
2. **Shadow one Sunday.** Sit next to an experienced operator for a full service. Watch what they press and when. Ask questions during the quiet parts.
3. **Run one Sunday with a trainer beside you.** This time you operate. The trainer watches and only steps in if needed.
4. **Get signed off.** The Livestream Lead goes through the [Skills Checklist](../training/skills-checklist.md) with you and signs it.

After sign-off, you can run Sundays on your own.
