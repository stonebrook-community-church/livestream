# Livestream Volunteer Training

Welcome to the livestream team. This site teaches you how to run the Sunday livestream. On Sunday, one person runs the whole stream alone, and after some training that person can be you.

You don't need any video experience. If you can use a computer, you can learn this. Every video term is explained the first time a page uses it, and [Video Basics](onboarding/video-basics.md) has pictures of all of them.

## The goal

**Every Sunday's livestream looks and runs the same, no matter who is operating.**

That is why the site has fixed camera shots, one button per scene, and a run sheet for every part of the service. You follow the same steps every week, and people watching at home get a calm, steady stream.

## The two parts of this site

1. **Onboarding guide.** This is the longer part. It explains the equipment, the shots, the scenes and the reasons behind each rule. Read it at home before your first Sunday.
2. **Booth reference sheets.** Short checklists and tables, each one fits on one printed page. Use them at the booth during the service.

The guide explains *why*. The sheets tell you *what to do next*.

## Good to know

- **This site is public.** It has no phone numbers, names, logins, passwords or stream keys. Those are on the **private booth card** at the booth.
- **The site describes the target setup.** Some cameras and scenes are still being set up to match it. Values that aren't decided yet say **TBD**.
- **The pictures are drawings for now.** Gray boxes that say "PHOTO NEEDED" will be replaced with real photos from the booth.

!!! tip "Something wrong on a Sunday?"
    Go to a safe scene and find the tech lead. See [When Things Go Wrong](onboarding/when-things-go-wrong.md).
